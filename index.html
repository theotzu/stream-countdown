Everything lives in the URL — bookmark it or paste it straight into an OBS Browser Source (1920×1080). Params: ?mode=soon|brb|ending&show=&headline=&sub=&d=YYYY-MM-DD&t=HH:MM&in=15&v=videoId&c=hexcolor&audio=1&vol=70&chart=1&csym=BINANCE:BTCUSDT&ci=3&transparent=1&nostars=1. chart=1 puts a live market behind the clock instead of the video, and pairs with audio=1 to keep the music. ci is the timeframe (5S, 60, 1D…) and ind the indicators (moonboys, rsi…). It is TradingView, not aggr — aggr refuses to be embedded anywhere; layer that one in OBS instead. Audio is off unless you ask for it. In OBS it starts on its own; in an ordinary browser tab the first click anywhere turns it on. Time is read in each viewer's own timezone - and labelled with it - unless you add &tz=-5 (UTC offset in hours), which pins it for everyone. Keys: S settings, F fullscreen, H hide hint.
